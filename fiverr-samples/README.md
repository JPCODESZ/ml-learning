# Portfolio samples

Two fixed-price samples a small business can read without a statistics background. Each folder has a script, a chart, and a short client report. The numbers in the reports come from running those scripts.

- [sales-forecast](sales-forecast/): monthly clothing-store sales. Public-domain U.S. Census data. Holt-Winters, chosen on an earlier window, beat a seasonal baseline on the held-out months.
- [churn-lead-scoring](churn-lead-scoring/): who to call first. UCI Bank Marketing data under CC BY 4.0. Gradient boosting beat logistic regression and a one-rule baseline.

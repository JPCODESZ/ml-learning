# Scoping questions

Draft only. Jon edits this and sends it himself.

---

A few questions so the job matches the decision, not just the file:

1. What will you do differently on Monday if the result is good?
2. What does one row represent: a day, an order, a customer, a lead?
3. Which column is the outcome, and which columns would you know before you act?
4. How far ahead do you need a forecast, or how many people can you actually contact?
5. What is worse: acting on someone who was never going to buy, or missing someone who would have?
6. Is there a column that is only filled in after the outcome (call length, final invoice, cancellation date)?
7. What would count as a useful result, in plain numbers you already track?

I will not treat a model as better than a simple rule unless it wins on months or customers it did not see during fitting.

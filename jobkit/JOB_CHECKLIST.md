# What the kit cannot do

The command writes a first draft. These steps are still Jon's. Do them before the file goes to a client. Nothing in this folder sends a message.

| Step | What to decide | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What decision will change if the number is useful? A forecast they will order against, or a list they will call? | 15 min |
| 2 | Confirm the target column, the date column, and what one row means. The kit only guesses. | 10 min |
| 3 | Read the leakage notes in `audit.json`. Put a column back only if the client would know it before the decision. | 20 min |
| 4 | Pick the business metric. Forecasts: average miss in money, or percent miss. Scoring: fewer wasted contacts, or fewer missed yeses. | 15 min |
| 5 | Edit every line in `REPORT.md` marked `REVIEW`. Delete anything you cannot explain out loud. | 20 min |
| 6 | Decide if the holdout result is good enough to deliver. If the model does not beat the baseline, say that. | 10 min |

About 90 minutes of judgment on a normal file. The kit does not know the client's costs, and it does not know if a column is fair to use.
